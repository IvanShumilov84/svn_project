Connect All

Use the Project / Connect All menu to connect or re-connect all the servers, channels and display controls in the project.

When the servers, channels and display controls are connected then data will be collected by the servers and passed to the other channels and display controls.  Data from display input controls will also be passed through to channels and server components.

When the components are not connected then no data will be passed around the components.

Normally the controls are connected automatically after loading the project and will remain connected.
