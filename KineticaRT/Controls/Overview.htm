The KineticaRT Slider is fast, easy to configure and supports live update. You can embed it straight into your application and its flexible look-and-feel makes it ideal for virtual control panels.

Manual input capability (mouse dragging)
Destination property allows connection to channel components
Source property allows feedback channel to automatically update display
Horizontal or vertical orientation with flexible movement options
Scale can show text, roman numerals etc. and display threshold marks


The KineticaRT Slider is a real-time multi-threaded control that can be embedded straight into your application or onto a KineticaRT Frame component to allow run-time configuration.

As with other KineticaRT controls width and height are configurable, as well as the colors of the separate parts of the slider.

This multi-purpose control has three different background options and several handle styles.

The slider can be configured for auto-return and to update continuously or only on completion of a mouse drag.

Orientation can be horizontal or vertical, with movement options being from top to bottom, bottom to top, left to right, right to left or centred.

Several fill styles are available including segmented and the position and width of the fill can be adjusted.
