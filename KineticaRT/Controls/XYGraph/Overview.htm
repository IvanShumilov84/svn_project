The XYGraph, in common with other KineticaRT components, is easy to configure and supports live update. You can embed it straight into your application and the flexible look-and-feel makes it ideal for virtual control panels. 

Multiple Traces
Separate left, right, top and bottom scales with optional threshold marks
Crosshairs
Source properties - Automatically update traces in real-time


The KineticaRT XYGraph is a real-time multi-threaded control that can be embedded straight into your application or onto a KineticaRT Frame component to allow run-time configuration.

As with other KineticaRT controls width and height are configurable, as well as the colors of the separate parts of the control.

Each of the multiple traces can have its own unique X and Y source connections, be individually scaled, be optionally faded over time and have its own pen style.
